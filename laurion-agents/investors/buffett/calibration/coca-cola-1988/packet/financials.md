# The Coca-Cola Company: financial summary, 1985-1988

$ millions except per share. Each year comes from that year's own annual report as originally reported: 1985-1986 from the 1986 report, 1987 from the 1987 report (dated 1988-02-18), 1988 from the 1988 report (chairman's letter signed 1989-02-18; probably not mailed before the as-of date, but the 1988 income figures were public in the 1989-01-31 earnings release). ## Restated bases

- The 1987 report restates 1985 and 1986 with the entertainment business deconsolidated (it became Columbia Pictures Entertainment, Inc., a 49%-owned affiliate, in 1987). Net income is unchanged; revenues and operating income are lower:

| | 1985 (restated) | 1986 (restated) | 1987 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net operating revenues | 5,879.2 | 6,976.6 | 7,658.3 |
| Gross profit | 2,969.7 | 3,522.7 | 4,025.2 |
| Operating income | 806.7 | 897.1 | 1,323.8 |
| Net income | 722.3 | 934.3 | 916.1 |

- The 1988 report restates the 1987 balance sheet to consolidate a finance subsidiary: total assets 8,605.5 (originally 8,355.6), shareholders' equity 3,187.2 (originally 3,223.8). 1987 revenue and net income are unchanged.

Not in this packet: 1988 quarterly reports (10-Q). Interim 1988 results appear only through the UPI story of 1988-07-14.
